## A. Главный промпт (видео 15 сек)

```text
Create a 15-second silent 3D animated comedy short for kids (ages 0+), in the exact visual style of the Korean series SEALOOK (Pinkfong / Million Volt): smooth matte vinyl toy-like 3D, soft global lighting, pastel colors, round blob characters, no dialogue, slapstick mime comedy only.

TITLE: HONEYLOOK / МЕДООК — Episode 1 “Банка” (The Jar). Exact runtime 15 seconds.

MAIN CHARACTER — keep consistent every frame:
A chubby honey badger that looks like a Sealook seal pup hybrid: head fused into a bean/blob body, very short stubby legs, tiny rounded ear nubs, soft matte fur (no realistic hair strands). Color: creamy silver-white cape from forehead down the entire back; deep charcoal-black face sides, belly, and legs. Face: ENORMOUS perfectly circular white eyeballs filling much of the face, TINY bright green irises with tiny black pupils (derpy vacant Sealook stare), small soft black button nose, 3 thin whiskers per side, occasional tiny pink blep tongue. Yellow rectangular wildlife tag “07” clipped on the left flank. Cute, funny, never scary.

SETTING: bright soft African savanna, sandy ground, gentle daylight, shallow depth of field, clean minimal background.

STORY BEATS (strict timing, one gag only):
0.0–1.0s — Title card “МЕДООК” with small subtitle “серия 1 · Банка”; soft bonk sound vibe; character frozen behind the title.
1.0–3.0s — Close-up: badger sniffs upward, ears twitch, huge eyes lock on something off-screen.
3.0–5.5s — Wide shot: exaggerated stealth waddle on short legs toward a clear glass honey jar on the sand.
5.5–7.5s — Nose poke into the jar; one beat of silence; jar SLAPS onto its snout like a helmet.
7.5–11.0s — Walks straight toward camera with the jar stuck on its head, stubby paws forward, slightly wobbly, proud silly energy.
11.0–13.0s — Soft BUMP into a tiny round cute bee (same Sealook eye style, yellow ball with thin black stripe, not scary); both shocked; a few honey drops drip from the jar.
13.0–14.5s — Hold: badger sits with jar still on head, tilts head “huh?”; bee mirrors the look; silence + tiny click.
14.5–15.0s — End card: yellow tag “07” and text “конец… пока”.

CAMERA: close-up (sniff) → wide (stealth) → push-in on jar-helmet walk → quick wide on bump → static hold → end card.

AUDIO: no speech; only soft cartoon SFX (sniff, tip-toe ticks, poke, slap, muffled footsteps, soft bump, tiny chime).

NEGATIVE / DO NOT:
no dialogue, no speech bubbles, no scary bees, no stingers, no blood, no realistic fur, no sharp teeth, no second gag, no dark horror mood, no purple neon look, no complex background clutter.
```
